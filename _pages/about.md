# About

**Ivana Ljubić** is Professor of Foundations of Operations Research at [WU Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://www.wu.ac.at/) in Vienna, Austria, a position she has held since September 2026. Prior to joining WU Vienna, she was Professor of Operations Research at [ESSEC Business School](https://www.essec.edu/) in Paris, where she was appointed in September 2015.

She obtained her habilitation in Operations Research from the [University of Vienna](https://www.univie.ac.at/en/) in 2013 and her PhD in Computer Science from [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/en/) in 2004. Before pursuing her academic career, she spent two years working in industry on portfolio optimization. She subsequently held an academic position at the University of Vienna until 2015. Throughout her career, she has also been a visiting researcher or professor at several leading institutions, including [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/pagina-strutturale/home), the [University of Maryland](https://umd.edu/), [TU Berlin](https://www.tu.berlin/en/), [TU Dortmund University](https://www.tu-dortmund.de/en/), and [Université Paris Dauphine–PSL](https://dauphine.psl.eu/en).

Ivana Ljubić has served on the Editorial Advisory Board of the [*European Journal of Operational Research*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/european-journal-of-operational-research) and is a member of the Editorial Advisory Board of [*Computers & Operations Research*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-operations-research). She is currently an **Area Editor for [*Transportation Science*](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/trsc)** and an **Associate Editor for [*Operations Research*](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/opre) and [*Networks*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10970037)**. She previously served as a guest editor for the *European Journal of Operational Research* and *Annals of Operations Research*. She also served as Chair of the [INFORMS Telecommunications Section](https://www.informs.org/Community/Telecom).

Her research focuses on network design, combinatorial optimization, optimization under uncertainty, and bilevel optimization. She develops and applies mathematical optimization methods, particularly mixed-integer linear and nonlinear programming, to challenging problems arising in telecommunications, transportation, logistics, network design, data and distribution networks, social networks, and bioinformatics.

Her research has been recognized through several fellowships and awards, including a Doctoral Fellowship of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/) (DOC, 2003–2004), the PhD Award of the [Austrian Society for Operations Research](https://www.asor.at/) (2005), a Hertha Firnberg Postdoctoral Fellowship of the [Austrian Science Fund (FWF)](https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/) (2007–2010), and an APART Fellowship of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (2011–2013).
